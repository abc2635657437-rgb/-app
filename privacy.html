隐私政策

Travel World（同路人） · 更新日期：2026 年 9 月 30 日

本政策说明 Travel World 网站如何处理登录、旅行规划与社交功能涉及的信息。隐私事项联系：abc2635657437@gmail.com。

1. 我们处理的信息与用途

注册和登录涉及邮箱、账号标识、昵称、头像及认证会话；邮箱密码认证由 Supabase Auth 处理。我们使用这些信息建立账号、验证身份、展示个人资料及维护登录状态。你提交的个人简介、帖子、照片、视频、评论、点赞、关注、收藏、路线与聊天内容用于实现你选择的功能。公开发布的内容和公开个人资料可被其他用户查看；聊天内容用于向会话成员提供消息服务。

2. Google 与 QQ 授权登录

Google 登录仅用于身份认证，申请 openid、email、profile 基本权限，接收账号标识、邮箱及基本资料（如昵称和头像）。不申请 Gmail 邮件、通讯录、Google Drive 或日历访问权限，也不会读取你的 Google 密码。认证由 Google 与 Supabase 完成；我们将认证结果用于创建或登录 Travel World 账号、匹配身份及防止重复账号。QQ 登录在开通后使用 QQ 账号标识、昵称和头像，不会获取 QQ 密码；QQ 未提供邮箱时，不会仅凭昵称自动合并其他账号。

Google 用户数据不用于广告画像、出售或 AI 模型训练。Travel World 对从 Google API 接收的信息的使用和向其他应用的转移遵守 Google API Services User Data Policy，包括 Limited Use 要求。

3. 位置与地图

定位需要浏览器授权。你主动开启位置共享时，我们会保存坐标、国家或城市及更新时间，并向地图用户显示所共享的位置和基本资料。关闭共享后，位置不再作为在线共享位置展示。你可在浏览器中撤销定位权限。搜索地点、查看地图或请求路线可能向地图、图片或路由服务发送相关地点或坐标。

4. AI、外部服务与数据传输

你主动使用 AI 助手或 AI 规划时，问题、旅行参数及为该功能提供的路线上下文会发送给对应 AI 服务；不要输入密码、身份证件、支付信息或他人的敏感资料。Google 登录资料不会作为 AI 请求输入。网站使用 Supabase 提供认证、数据库和媒体存储，Render 提供网站托管；外部地图、图片、AI 页面和授权页面的服务商也按各自政策处理访问信息。你的请求可能跨境传输或在境外服务器处理；网络服务商可能处理 IP、设备和请求日志以运行及保护服务。

5. 本地存储与安全

浏览器本地存储用于登录会话、语言和货币偏好及页面缓存；服务缓存用于加载资源。QQ 授权使用短期状态 Cookie 防止伪造回调。退出登录可清除本设备的应用登录状态，清除浏览器网站数据可移除本地缓存。会话令牌属于敏感凭据，请勿在共享设备上保留登录。我们使用 HTTPS、服务端鉴权和数据库访问控制保护信息；没有任何网络系统能保证绝对安全。

6. 保存期限、删除与选择权

账号、你保存或发布的内容通常保留以提供相应功能，直到你删除内容或申请删除账号。备份、服务商日志及法律或安全处理所需的记录可能不会立即同步删除；目前不承诺统一的自动清除天数。你可修改个人资料、删除网站提供删除入口的内容、关闭位置共享、退出登录，并在 Google 账号的第三方连接设置中撤销授权；撤销 Google 授权不等同于删除 Travel World 中的已有数据。

申请访问、更正、导出或删除账号及相关数据，请使用注册邮箱联系上述地址；我们会核实身份、说明处理范围和结果。QQ 用户可提供 Travel World 账号标识协助核实，请勿发送密码或验证码。依法需要保留的信息、其他用户独立发布的内容，以及已由其他用户保存的副本可能不在可立即删除范围内。

7. 未成年人及政策更新

未成年人应在监护人指导下使用网站，不应公开精确位置或敏感个人信息。监护人可联系上述邮箱提出相关数据处理请求。政策变化会在本页更新日期和内容；涉及新的数据用途或权限时，应另行提供说明及必要的选择。

Privacy Policy

Travel World · Updated September 30, 2026

This policy describes information processed by Travel World for sign-in, travel planning and social features. Privacy contact: abc2635657437@gmail.com.

1. Information and purposes

Registration and sign-in involve email, account identifiers, display name, avatar and authentication sessions. Supabase Auth processes email/password authentication. We use these details to create accounts, authenticate users, show profiles and maintain sessions. Profile biographies, posts, photos, videos, comments, likes, follows, saved items, routes and messages you submit support the features you choose. Public posts and public profiles are visible to other users. Messages are used to provide messaging to conversation members.

2. Google and QQ sign-in

Google sign-in requests only basic openid, email and profile permissions for authentication. We receive account identifiers, email and basic profile details such as name and avatar. We do not request Gmail, contacts, Drive or calendar access, and never read your Google password. Google and Supabase perform authentication. We use the result to create or sign in to a Travel World account, associate identities and avoid duplicate accounts. Once available, QQ sign-in uses account identifiers, nickname and avatar, not your QQ password. Without a verified email, QQ accounts are not automatically merged based on nickname.

Google user data is not used for advertising profiles, sold or used to train AI models. Travel World's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

3. Location and maps

Location requires browser permission. When you actively enable location sharing, we store coordinates, country or city and update time, and display the shared location and basic profile to map users. Disabling sharing stops its display as an online shared location. You can revoke browser location permission. Place searches, maps and routing requests may send places or coordinates to map, image or routing services.

4. AI, providers and international processing

When you choose AI assistance or planning, your questions, trip parameters and route context provided for that feature are sent to the relevant AI service. Do not submit passwords, identity documents, payment information or others' sensitive data. Google sign-in profile data is not sent as AI prompt input. Supabase provides authentication, database and media storage; Render hosts the website. External map, image, AI and authorization providers also process visits under their own policies. Requests may be transferred internationally or processed on overseas servers. Network providers may process IP, device and request logs to operate and secure services.

5. Browser storage and security

Browser storage keeps authentication sessions, language and currency preferences, and page caches. Resource caches support loading. QQ authorization uses a short-lived state cookie to protect callbacks. Signing out clears this device's application sign-in state; clearing website data removes local caches. Session tokens are sensitive credentials: avoid remaining signed in on shared devices. HTTPS, server-side authentication and database access controls protect information, but no network system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention, deletion and choices

Accounts and saved or published content generally remain to provide the features until you delete content or request account deletion. Backups, provider logs and records required for legal or security purposes may not be deleted immediately. We do not currently promise a single automatic deletion period. You can edit your profile, delete content where deletion controls are provided, disable location sharing, sign out and revoke Google authorization in your Google account's third-party connections settings. Revoking Google access does not itself delete existing Travel World data.

For access, correction, export or account and related data deletion requests, contact the address above from your registered email. We will verify identity and explain the scope and outcome. QQ users may provide their Travel World account identifier to assist verification; never send passwords or verification codes. Legally required records, content independently published by others and copies saved by others may not be immediately deletable.

7. Minors and updates

Minors should use the site with guardian guidance and avoid sharing precise locations or sensitive personal information. Guardians may contact us about data requests. Changes will appear on this page with an updated date; new purposes or permissions should receive additional disclosure and appropriate choices.
